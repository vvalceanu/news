Level A2: simplify vocabulary only. Grammar stays natural and unrestricted: keep any tense, mood, passive, subordinate clauses and sentence structure a native writer would use.
Use the most common everyday German words, roughly the scope of the Goethe-Zertifikat A2 word list, and prefer concrete words over abstract ones.
Replace rare, technical or bureaucratic words, long compound nouns and Amtsdeutsch with a common word or a short paraphrase (e.g. "Ermittlungsverfahren" becomes "die Polizei prüft den Fall").
Keep proper names of people, places and organisations unchanged.
Prefer idiomatic, natural German collocations over literal or clumsy simplifications.
